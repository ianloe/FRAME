**Version 1.0**
**Author: Ian Loe, Tactics Solutions**

---

## How to Use This Checklist

This checklist is the operational companion to the FRAME Practitioner Guide. It provides a structured record of governance completion for a single AI deployment, covering both pre-deployment requirements and ongoing operational governance.

Complete Section 1 before deployment. A deployment with incomplete Section 1 items should not enter production.

Complete Section 2 items on the defined schedule once the system is in production.

Retain completed checklists as part of the governance documentation package for this deployment.
